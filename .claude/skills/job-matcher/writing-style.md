# Writing Style

Use British English for job-search output. No em dash characters. En dashes are allowed only where naturally appropriate, such as date ranges.

Write confidently, directly, and specifically. Do not invent claims, metrics, tools, seniority, responsibilities, outcomes, or sponsorship facts. Prefer named systems, real technologies, actual thresholds, defensible numbers, and concrete evidence.

Avoid generic AI-sounding filler, repeated template rhythm, rule-of-three padding, and job-description parroting. Keep screening and ranking output compact enough to support a decision quickly.
