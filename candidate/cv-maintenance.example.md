# CV Maintenance Example

Optional private rules for keeping a master CV aligned with the candidate profile.

- Keep official employment titles factual.
- Do not invent metrics, responsibilities, tools, dates, outcomes, or seniority.
- Keep the master CV general rather than rewriting it for a single vacancy.
- Define page-length, writing-style, and formatting rules appropriate to the candidate.
- Record private visa/location decisions here rather than in public project files.
